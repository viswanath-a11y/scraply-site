Scraply

A clean, private photo journal for Android.

Scraply lets you organize your photos into journal folders, write memories alongside them, and keep everything safely backed up — encrypted, and stored in your own Google Drive, not ours.

Your memories, your Drive

Photos and journal entries are encrypted on your device and backed up to your own hidden Google Drive folder — never a server we control.

Private by design

Your journal content is encrypted before it ever leaves your device. Nobody but you can read it.

Works across devices

Sign in with the same Google account anywhere and your journal restores automatically.
