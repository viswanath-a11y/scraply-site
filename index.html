Scraply

A clean, private photo journal app for Android.

Scraply is an Android app that helps you keep a photo journal. You organize your photos into dated folders, write personal memories alongside each one, and Scraply automatically keeps everything backed up — encrypted, and stored in your own Google Drive account, not on our servers. If you get a new phone or reinstall the app, signing back in with the same Google account restores your entire journal automatically.

Your memories, your Drive

Photos and journal entries are encrypted on your device and backed up to your own hidden Google Drive folder — never a server we control.

Private by design

Your journal content is encrypted before it ever leaves your device. Nobody but you can read it.

Works across devices

Sign in with the same Google account anywhere and your journal restores automatically.
