Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Scraply collects, how it's used, and — just as importantly — what we deliberately do not do with your data.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics — name, email address, and profile photo, obtained when you sign in with Google. Used only to identify your account and let you sign in again on another device.
  • A minimal account record — an internal account identifier and whether you've completed initial setup. Stored in our backend (Supabase) purely to make sign-in work.

Your journal content — photos and memories

Your photos and journal entries are encrypted on your device, before they're ever uploaded anywhere, and stored in a hidden folder inside your own Google Drive account — not on our servers, and not visible in your regular Drive. We do not, and cannot, read this content. The encryption key is generated randomly per account, never derived from anything guessable, and is itself stored in a way that only your signed-in account can retrieve it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks embedded in the app.
  • No analytics or usage tracking beyond what's strictly needed to make the app function.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties.
  • Your journal content is never accessible to us — it's encrypted before it leaves your device.

Google Drive access

Scraply requests access to a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope) — this folder is not visible in your normal Drive interface and cannot be browsed or accessed by any other app. It's used solely to store your encrypted backup.

Data retention and deletion

Your journal content lives in your own Google Drive for as long as you keep the app installed and signed in. To request deletion of your account record from our backend, contact us at the email below — we'll remove it promptly. Removing the app's access to your Drive can also be done directly from your Google Account's security settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a revised date at the top.
